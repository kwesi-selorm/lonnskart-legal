4. Initial Seed Data and Sources

To launch Lonnskart with meaningful salary insights, we collected initial seed data from publicly available sources. We believe in complete transparency about where this data comes from and how we collected it.

4.1 Overview of Initial Dataset

Our initial database contains 151 verified salary entries collected from publicly shared salary information on platforms where professionals voluntarily disclosed their salaries. This seed data provides:

  • 27 countries represented across 5 continents
  • 21 currencies including NOK, USD, EUR, GBP, and more
  • 13 job categories from technology to healthcare to education
  • Norwegian focus: 34 entries (22.5% of dataset) from Norway covering Oslo, Bergen, Trondheim, and Stavanger
  • Time period: 2020-2025, with most entries from 2024-2025

4.2 Data Sources

We collected salary data only from publicly available sources where professionals voluntarily shared their salary information:

  • Reddit: Salary transparency communities including r/cscareerquestions, r/nursing, r/teachers, r/norge, and other career-focused subreddits
  • Levels.fyi: Public tech salary data shared by professionals
  • Glassdoor: Verified employee salary reports
  • Blind: Anonymous professional community salary discussions
  • Norwegian platforms: Kode24 (tech salary discussions), Norwegian Reddit communities, and tech Slack communities
  • LinkedIn Salary Insights: Publicly available salary ranges

4.3 Collection Methodology

Our approach to collecting seed data was designed with privacy and accuracy as top priorities:

  • Public sources only: We only collected salaries that people chose to share publicly on salary transparency platforms
  • Full anonymization: All data was anonymized during collection—no names, company names, email addresses, or other identifying information was collected
  • Manual verification: Each entry was manually reviewed and cross-referenced against market data for reasonableness
  • Geographic limitation: Location data is limited to city level only
  • Quality control: We removed outliers and entries that couldn't be verified against multiple sources

4.4 Verification Process

Every entry in our seed dataset underwent rigorous verification:

  • Cross-referenced against market salary data for each role and location
  • Validated that job titles match our standardized categories
  • Confirmed currencies align with countries and regions
  • Verified years of experience correlate reasonably with salary levels
  • Removed statistically unlikely entries (outliers more than 30% above/below market averages)
  • Ensured compliance with our job category taxonomy (35 categories) and industry classification (114 sectors)

4.5 Privacy Protection for Seed Data

Important: The seed data is subject to the same privacy protections as user-contributed data:

  • No personal identifiable information included
  • No company names or identifying details
  • Only publicly shared information used
  • Full GDPR compliance maintained
  • Location limited to city level only

4.6 Norwegian Market Coverage

We paid special attention to collecting comprehensive Norwegian salary data:

  • 34 verified entries from Norway (2020-2025)
  • Cities covered: Oslo, Bergen, Trondheim, Stavanger
  • Primary sectors: Technology, Healthcare, Education
  • Example salary ranges:
    • Junior Software Engineers: ~655,000 NOK
    • Software Engineers: 650,000 - 970,000 NOK
    • Senior Software Engineers: 720,000 - 920,000 NOK
    • Registered Nurses: 510,000 - 630,000 NOK
    • Teachers: 450,000 - 510,000 NOK

4.7 Limitations and Disclaimers

We believe in honest communication about the limitations of our seed data:

  • Sample size: 151 entries is our foundation—some roles and locations have limited representation
  • Self-reported data: All salaries are self-reported by individuals; we verify for reasonableness but cannot independently confirm every submission
  • Time periods: Data spans 2020-2025; older entries may not reflect current market rates
  • Geographic bias: Heavy concentration in US (36.4%) and Norwegian (22.5%) markets
  • Role coverage: Tech roles are overrepresented due to active online salary discussions on platforms like Reddit

4.8 Ethical Data Collection

Our approach respects individual privacy and follows the spirit of salary transparency movements:

  • We only use publicly shared information where individuals voluntarily disclosed their salaries
  • We don't scrape private company data or require unauthorized access
  • We respect GDPR and Norwegian data protection laws
  • We prioritize quality over quantity—manually reviewing each entry rather than scraping thousands of unverified entries

4.9 Growing the Database

The 151 seed entries are just our foundation. Lonnskart's true value comes from community contributions—your salary submissions. Every verified salary you add:

  • Helps professionals in your field make informed decisions
  • Increases transparency in the Norwegian and global job markets
  • Creates better negotiation power for everyone
  • Is completely anonymous and secure

We will not continue to add seed data from external sources. All future data growth comes from users like you.

  • Legitimate Interests: We have a legitimate interest in preventing fraud, ensuring data quality, and improving our services (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)
  • Legal Obligations: In some cases, we may need to process data to comply with legal requirements (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR)

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or contacting us.

6. How We Protect Anonymity

Lonnskart is designed with privacy as a core principle:

6.1 Anonymous Contributions

  • Salary submissions are not directly linked to your account in our database
  • We use cryptographic hashing to create pseudonymous contributor identifiers
  • Other users cannot see who submitted specific salary data

6.2 Aggregation and Privacy Protection

  • We aggregate data to prevent identification of individuals
  • In cases where salary data might identify someone (e.g., unique job titles in small companies), we may exclude or generalize that data
  • We do not share raw individual submissions with third parties

6.3 Limitations of Anonymity

Important: While we take extensive measures to protect anonymity, you should be aware that:

  • Highly specific combinations of metadata (e.g., rare job title + specific location + uncommon experience level) could potentially be identifying
  • You should avoid including identifying information in free-text fields
  • True anonymity cannot be guaranteed if you choose to share very unique data points

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for different periods depending on the type:

  • Account data: Retained as long as your account is active. The account is deleted immediately after deletion
  • Salary submissions: Retained indefinitely to maintain the integrity of our salary database, unless you request deletion
  • Analytics data: Aggregated and anonymized data is retained indefinitely; individual session data is retained for 7 days. Revoked sessions are deleted daily
  • Logs and technical data: Retained for 90 days for security and troubleshooting purposes

You can request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 11).
