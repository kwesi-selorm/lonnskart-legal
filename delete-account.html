Delete Your Account

Last Updated: March 17, 2026

What You Should Know

  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
  • Your personal information is deleted immediately upon request
  • Anonymized salary contributions may be retained to preserve data integrity
  • You can delete your account directly from within the app
  • Alternatively, you can contact us to request deletion by email

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your Lonnskart account at any time using either of the two methods below. Deletion is immediate — once confirmed, your account cannot be recovered.

Option 1: Delete from within the app (recommended)

Follow these steps in the Lonnskart app:

1
Open the app and go to your Account

Tap the top-left menu icon on the Explore screen to open the side drawer, then tap Account.

2
Tap Delete Account button

On the bottom of the Settings screen you will find a list of account action buttons, tap Delete Account.

3
Confirm deletion

Read the confirmation prompt carefully and confirm. Your account will be deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us at support@lonnskart.com from the email address associated with your account. Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line. We will process your request within 30 days.

What Data Gets Deleted

Deleted immediately

  • Your account credentials and authentication data
  • Your email address and any personal identifiers
  • All sessions activities

Retained after deletion

Lonnskart is a community-driven salary transparency platform. Salary contributions are anonymized before being stored — they are not linked to your identity in any way. To preserve the integrity of the platform's data for the wider community, anonymized salary entries you have submitted may be retained after your account is deleted.

Note: Retained salary data contains no personal identifiers. It cannot be traced back to you after your account is deleted. It is used solely to provide salary insights to other professionals on the platform.

Retention Period

Personal data (email, credentials) is deleted immediately upon account deletion. Anonymized salary contributions have no fixed deletion date, as they form part of the platform's aggregated dataset and contain no personal information.

If you have specific concerns about data retention, please contact us at support@lonnskart.com.

Deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered. If you wish to use Lonnskart again in the future, you will need to create a new account.

Further Information

For more information about how Lonnskart handles your data, please review our full Privacy Policy. For general questions about your rights or our data practices, you can also reach us via the Support screen within the app.

Need Help?

Email: support@lonnskart.com

In-App: Use the Support screen in Settings or Profile
